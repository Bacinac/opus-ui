Player's **Settings** are what belongs to the person in front of the screen. What describes the machine or the rest of the house is on a separate page, **Server and profiles**, which only the admin sees.

## Your settings

- The language and look of the interface.
- **My profile**: the languages of sound and subtitles in order (**First**, **Then**) and whose photographs the screensaver shows.
- **Listening and watching history**: linking ListenBrainz for music and Simkl for films and series. Linking also sends everything you have listened to or watched so far.
- **Subtitle appearance**: size, place and backdrop. It holds for this device.
- **Vault**: your private photographs; see [Photographs](/help/photos).
- **Android app** and **OPUS Music for the car**: downloaded by scanning a code with the phone's camera. OPUS Music brings the music and radio into Android Auto; the linked cars are listed here too.
- **Switch profile** and **Sign out**.

## Server and profiles

- **Disk space**: how much is free on the folders of music, films, series, photographs, videos and downloads.
- **Profiles**: every profile of the household with its languages and screensaver. **Add a profile** makes one for somebody with no account, and such a profile can also be removed. Profiles with an account come from Library; see [Roles, profiles and devices](/help/roles).
- **Televisions**: which receiver input each box's picture and sound arrive on. A film on a box turns the receiver to that input, for as long as the box stays plugged in where it was when the input was chosen.
- **Server settings**: how the home automation Player drives the devices through is reached, where sound that is not playing on the screen goes (stereo to the DAC, multichannel to the receiver), the install's Simkl app and the module tokens.
