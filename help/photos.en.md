Photographs are neither copied nor moved. Library reads them where they are, in the household's folder, which is mounted read-only, so not even a fault in the program can change them. The thumbnails and previews shown are made in a separate folder, which is only a cache and needs no backup.

That folder is written to in only two ways: when somebody sends photographs from their phone to the family album, and when somebody offers a photograph from their vault. A new photograph then goes into the folder of the year and month it was taken.

## From a face to a person

Faces are looked for on every photograph, and similar faces are gathered into groups by how they look. A group is not yet a person: the machine sees a likeness, and who it is, a human knows. The admin names a person in Library, under **People**, by answering **Who is this?**. A name once given counts for more than any likeness.

A child of three and of thirteen do not look alike, so groups are gathered within a period, and a person is a run of such groups across the years. A few rules keep other people's faces out of a person:

- two faces on the same photograph are never the same person;
- once a birth date is known, a group with photographs older than it cannot belong to that person;
- **Not this person** takes a group off a person that it does not belong to, and its faces stay for the right name.

## Family

A person with an account in the household is family by that account, and anyone else can be marked **Family**. Only family may appear on the screensaver.

## The private vault

Every user has a vault of their own for the photographs on their phone. Everything is encrypted on the device before it is sent, so neither the server nor the admin can open it. That is why a vault has no search, no face recognition and no timeline: those are done by the server, and the server sees nothing in a vault.

A vault is opened with your password or with its **recovery code**. Write the code down and keep it away from the phone. If both the password and the code are lost, nobody can bring the photographs back. A photograph in the vault can be **offered to the family**: a copy of it joins the shared album, and it stays in the vault as well.

## Sharing by link

The admin can share chosen photographs through a link that lasts a set number of days. Whoever has the link sees those photographs and can download the originals, but without the location, the date, the device and the names of the people. Videos are not shared. The link is shown only once and can be revoked.
