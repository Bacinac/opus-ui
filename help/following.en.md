The **Library** page shows the library one kind at a time: the kind strip picks music, films, series, videos or photographs. The numbers beside a kind say how much there is, how much is complete, and how much is wanted, waiting for subtitles or incomplete.

## Search

The **Search** page searches one kind's catalogue, because music is looked for by artist and a film by its title. Music is searched in Deezer's catalogue by artist or song, and films and series in TMDB by title. Web video is not searched for but added: on the Library page, under the **Video** kind, paste the link of a video, a channel or a playlist.

## Following

What is followed arrives on its own. Library goes through everything followed every six hours.

- An **artist** is added with **Add to library** and followed from then on. Library keeps their discography in step and fetches only a release that is really newly released, within the last 90 days. Older albums are not fetched on their own; a single album is fetched on the artist's page.
- A **film** added to the library is looked for on every pass, until a release appears that passes the check.
- A **series** is followed with **Monitor series**. New seasons and episodes come from TMDB, and following can be switched on for a single season. **Download season** takes a whole season at once.
- A web video **channel** is followed as soon as it is added, and its new videos are fetched on their own.

## An artist's page

An artist's page lists every release with its state and quality. From there an album is fetched, a candidate picked by hand (**Pick…**), an incomplete album replaced with a whole one and a lossy album with FLAC. Beside the stereo edition, a surround or DSD edition can be looked for. **Retag all files** writes the catalogue's values into the files' tags. An artist whose identity is not settled on Wikidata has a field for linking it by hand.

## What you already have

**Scan** goes through the existing folder and links its files to the catalogue. The scan report says what was linked, what only in part, and why something was not: no tags, the artist not recognised, the album not found. Folders left unlinked stay listed for review and linking by hand.
