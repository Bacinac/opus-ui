The **Photos** section is the household's family album. A guest does not see it.

## Ways of looking

- **Once**: photographs taken on this day in earlier years. The section opens here.
- **Years**: every photograph in order of time.
- **People**: each person across the years.
- **Places**: by the country and place where they were taken.
- **Quiz**: a photograph from the archive, four answers and twenty seconds. Only what is known about the photograph is asked: who is in it, where it was taken and in which year.
- **Search**: a search of the photographs.

## From the phone to the album

The menu beside your name has **Share photos**: the photographs chosen on the phone are sent to the family album. The Android app adds OPUS to the share menu, so photographs go straight from the gallery. What is already in the album is recognised and not sent again.

Photographs that are yours alone go into the vault, on the **Settings** page; see [Photographs](/help/photos).

## To the television

An open photograph has **To the television**: it appears in OPUS on the television, and what you swipe through on the phone moves on the television too. While a photograph is on the television, the screen stays awake.

## Screensaver

When the television is idle, the screensaver shows photographs of the people the profile chose, or of the whole family. Only people who are family appear on the screensaver.
