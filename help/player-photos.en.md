The **Photos** section is the household's family album. A guest sees it only once it is set whose photographs they see, and then only those; see [Roles, profiles and devices](/help/roles).

## Ways of looking

- **Once**: photographs taken on this day in earlier years. The section opens here.
- **Years**: every photograph in order of time.
- **People**: each person across the years.
- **Places**: by the country and place where they were taken.
- **Quiz**: a game over the archive; see below.
- **Search**: a search of the photographs.

## Quiz

A photograph from the archive, four answers and twenty seconds. Only what is known about the photograph is asked:

- who is in it, where it was taken and in which year;
- who is missing: one person is covered, and the answers on offer are those most often photographed with the others in the picture;
- where this is: a detail of the photograph that widens as the clock runs;
- which of two photographs was taken first;
- how old somebody was, when Library knows their birthday.

**Difficulty** (**Easy**, **Fair**, **Hard**) sets how clear the faces in the questions are. **Together** turns the quiz into a party game: this screen asks and keeps the clock, and everyone answers on their own phone, through Photos → Quiz → **Join**. Each player's round counts in the **Standings** like one played alone.

## From the phone to the album

The menu beside your name has **Share photos**: the photographs chosen on the phone are sent to the family album. The Android app adds OPUS to the share menu, so photographs go straight from the gallery. What is already in the album is recognised and not sent again.

Photographs that are yours alone go into the vault, on the **Settings** page; see [Photographs](/help/photos).

## To the television

An open photograph has **To the television**: it appears in OPUS on the television, and what you swipe through on the phone moves on the television too. While a photograph is on the television, the screen stays awake.

## Screensaver

When the television is idle, the screensaver shows what the profile chose: photographs of the profile's own person, of somebody else or of the whole family, or the wall of **The cameras**. Only people who are family appear on the screensaver.
